Terrain rules applied

Woods / Building — Obstacle (blocks if touched at or above viewer height)
Hedge / Wall hexside — Obstacle (blocks outright), but clear if the hexside is one of the firer's or target's own six hexsides
Hills — a Hill blocks LOS between two lower hexes [T88.3.3]; between different elevations any intervening crest at/above the higher hex blocks [T88.3.2]; a lower Woods/Building creates exactly one Blind Hex immediately behind it [T88.4.1]
Fence hexside / Field — Hindrance −1 FP (Fence exempt on the firer's/target's own hexsides)
Orchard — Hindrance −2 FP
Brush — Hindrance −3 FP
Hindrances are never cumulative – only the single largest applies [10.3.3]. Hindrances at a lower elevation than a Hill hex don't affect LOS to/from it [T88.5]. Terrain in the sighting/target hex itself never affects LOS [10.1].
